Borsa İstanbul — Weight of the Top 10 in XU100

Share of total free-float market cap held by the 10 largest members · monthly, recomputed each date · as of

Top-10 concentration

Cross-sectional each month: the 10 largest members on that date ÷ all XU100 members that date, by free-float market cap. Not a fixed basket — membership changes over time (see strip below).

Who sat in the top 10

Two snapshots a year (January & end of H1); rows are rank 1 (top) to 10. Colour = company. The banks → defence/aviation/industrials shift is the story.

Method & caveats

Weighting basis: free-float market cap (İş Yatırım HAO_PD), the basis BIST index-weights on — not full market cap.
Not a tracked basket: W(t) is recomputed cross-sectionally each month; there is no entry/exit logic. A company can enter or leave the top 10 as caps move.
Survivorship (read this before the early years): the universe is today's XU100 applied historically, so it grows from to members (dashed gold line) as current constituents list over time. A smaller early pool mechanically inflates the top-10 share — 10 of ~65 names is a bigger slice than 10 of 100 — so the elevated pre-2021 readings overstate true concentration; the decline toward ~48% is partly the universe filling out, not pure de-concentration. Treat the recent (near-100-member) years as the clean signal and read the earlier years directionally only. Reconstructing the true historical XU100 membership would need Borsa İstanbul's paid archive.
