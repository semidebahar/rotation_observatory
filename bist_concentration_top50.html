Method & caveats

Weighting basis: free-float market cap (İş Yatırım HAO_PD), the basis BIST index-weights on — not full market cap.
Fixed top-50 denominator (the point of this variant): the denominator is the 50 largest members each date, not "all available members". Over 10y the observable universe grows from ~65 to 100 (today's XU100 applied historically), and a variable-size denominator mechanically inflates the top-10 share in the thin early years. A constant top-50 puts every date on identical footing, so the trend is comparable end-to-end.
Why 50, not 65 (the min universe): the top 50 keeps a ~15-name bench in the worst year. When a name that truly belonged in the top 50 is one of the survivorship-missing tickers, its slot is filled by the next survivor down — substitution (small, second-order error) rather than omission (large, first-order). At N=65 the bench is zero and omission bias returns exactly where it hurts. Residual denominator error is bounded, one-sided, and decays to ~0 by ~2021.
Numerator is essentially exact in every year: 2016's top-10 names (GARAN, AKBNK, ISCTR, TUPRS, THYAO, EREGL, KCHOL…) are all still in today's list — megacaps persist, so the top of the distribution is fully observed across the window.
What the empirics actually showed (correcting the prior story): fixing the denominator at top-50 did not flatten the early peak — W50 still reads ~62% in 2016–19. So that early concentration was largely real, not a survivorship/denominator-size artifact, as first feared. The extra steepness of the old "% of total" chart (which fell from ~61% to ~48%) is mostly the small-cap tail growing — more listings and the 2021–22 retail episode diluting the total — not core de-concentration. Within the core, the fade is milder: ~62% → ~55%.
Where the real survivorship bias sits: in the ~35 names missing from 2016 (a tail we cannot observe). It pushes the old of-total reading up in early years, but it is not the blue–grey gap on the chart. Backing it out: true 2016 of-total ≈ W50 × ρ_recent ≈ 0.62 × 0.88 ≈ 55%, versus the naive 61% — i.e. the old chart's 2016 point was ~6pp too high.
Definitional caveat (not error): W50 is blind to ranks 51–100 (~12% of total cap today). Read it as concentration within the large/mid-cap core. Level note: W50 = W(of-total) / ρ, ρ ≈ 0.88 today, so the same object reads a few points higher here (latest 55% vs 48%).
